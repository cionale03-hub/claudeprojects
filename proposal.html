Do you need help with Bathing?*

If the member just needs a reminder to bathe, choose "Low (15m - Verbal assist)".
If the member needs some physical assistance with bathing, choose "Moderate (30m - Some assist)".
If the member needs total assistance with bathing, choose "High (45m - Total assist)".

Days per week bathing assistance is needed.*

Do you need help with Grooming?*

  • Does veteran need verbal reminders to brush their teeth, comb their hair, etc.?
  • Does the member need physical assistance with brushing their teeth, combing their hair, etc.?
If the member just needs a verbal reminder to brush their teeth, combing their hair, etc., choose "Low (15m - Verbal assist)".
If the member needs some physical assistance with brushing teeth, combing hair, etc., choose "Moderate (30m - Some assist)".
If the member needs total assistance with brushing teeth, combing hair, etc., choose "High (45m - Total assist)".

Toileting:*

  • Do you have any problems getting to the bathroom and using the toilet?
  • Do you use incontinence supplies, and do you need assistance?
  • Do you need assistance changing or cleaning yourself? (If YES, Select Total Assistance)
If the member just needs minimal help when toileting, choose "Low (15m - Verbal assist)".
If the member answers yes to part one and two of these questions, choose "Moderate (30m - Some assist)".

Transferring and Mobility:*

  • Can you get in and out of bed or chair?
  • Are you able to walk without help? Do you have problems with your balance?
If the member only needs help getting in and out of their bed or chair, choose "Low (15m - Verbal Assist)".
If the member needs help getting in and out of their bed/chair and has problems with their balance, choose "Moderate (30m - Moderate Assist)".
If the client needs assistance with all actions and are unable to walk without help, choose "High (45m - Total Assist)".
